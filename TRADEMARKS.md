# The name and the logo

This policy says what anyone may do with the name Tabachir and its logo. The code and the documents are free to use under their licences. The name and the logo are not covered by those licences ([PRD §1.9](docs/prd/PRD.md#19-official-builds-releases-the-name-and-security)).

## The name

- **Tabachir (طباشير)** means chalk, the teacher's everyday tool.
- **Spelling.** Always "Tabachir" in Latin letters, never "Tabashir". The Arabic form is طباشير.
- **Not registered.** The name and the logo are not registered as trademarks. They are protected by use, by the official sources below and by the published checksums.
- **Who owns them.** The founder, until a legal entity exists. They then move to the association ([PRD §1.3](docs/prd/PRD.md#13-licences)).

## Official builds

- **Official sources:** Google Play, the project's `.dz` website (the Android APK, and the PC and web apps) and F-Droid. No other source is official.
- **There is no app yet.** Any app called Tabachir today is not the project's.
- **Signed and checkable.** Every release is signed. The signing key's fingerprint and each release's checksums are published on the website and in the [README](README.md).

## What anyone may do

- **Share unmodified official builds,** as they are.
- **Fork the code** under its licences, with another name and logo.
- **Name Tabachir truthfully,** for example in an article, a review or a tutorial, or to say that a fork is based on Tabachir's code.

## What needs another name

- **A modified app or server.** A fork uses its own name and logo, and nothing in it may suggest that it is the official app or that the project endorses it.
- **Look-alikes.** No name or logo that could be confused with Tabachir's.

## The Ministry's app

In the national system, the Ministry's app is the project's release, with the Ministry's name and icon as settings. It runs under the Ministry's own name, and only the project's builds are called Tabachir. It is built reproducibly, so anyone can check it against the published code, and the project's own app can always connect to the national system ([PRD §5.2](docs/prd/PRD.md#52-architecture)).

## Other countries

Only an official country edition, decided in a decision record, may use the name in another country ([PRD §1.14](docs/prd/PRD.md#114-algeria-first-flexible-for-other-countries)). A fork for another country uses another name.

## Questions and misuse

Ask a question, or report a misuse of the name, in a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/founderscoffee/tabachir/issues).
