# 0004. The founder holds the copyright and the trademark until a legal entity exists

- **Date:** 2026-09-26
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decided by:** the founder

## Context

Someone must own the name and logo, and the copyright in the founder's own work. No legal entity exists yet.

## Options

- The founder, for now.
- A company or auto-entrepreneur status, which is needed anyway to sell services.
- An association.

## Decision

Until a legal entity exists, the founder holds the copyright in their own work and owns the name and logo. Each contributor keeps the copyright in their contribution. When an entity is created, the founder's rights move to it.

## Consequences

- The choice of legal entity is still open. It is needed to sell services and to receive grants.
- The trademark is filed with INAPI through counsel.
