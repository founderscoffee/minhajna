# 00NN. The decision, in a few words

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Decided by:** who decides, for example the lead maintainer

## Context

What makes a decision necessary now: the facts, the constraints, and the principles and PRD sections involved.

## Options

- **An option.** What it means, and its main advantage and cost.
- **Another option.** What it means, and its main advantage and cost. Chosen.

## Decision

- What was decided, point by point.

Details: [PRD §N](../prd/PRD.md), if the PRD holds them.

## Consequences

- What becomes easier or harder, what must be done next, and what stays open.
