# 0006. Charge for services, never for features

- **Date:** 2026-09-26
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decided by:** the founder

## Context

Under an open licence, anyone may legally rebuild the app without a paywall. Google Play can't bill Algerians, so a paid feature would have to be an unlock key sold through a local payment channel. A free rebuild would then spread through the same teacher groups.

## Options

- Paid features unlocked by keys.
- Open core, with closed paid modules. This contradicts [0001](0001-open-source-from-the-first-line.md).
- Free features, with paid services.

## Decision

- **Always free:**
  - every feature of the app, including the term export and every print layout;
  - exporting a teacher's own data.
- **Paid services:**
  - end-to-end encrypted sync and backup between phone and PC, hosted in Algeria;
  - deployment, training and support for schools, directorates and, later, the ministry.
- **Voluntary:** a supporter pass, which locks nothing.
- **Grants and sponsors:** accepted only if they respect every principle, and disclosed publicly.
- **Never:** ads, selling or sharing data, paid features, or charging teachers to get their own data out.

## Consequences

- Payment goes only through channels approved in Algeria, and billing data is kept apart from everything else.
- Prices are set in the business section of the PRD.
- Teacher payments alone will not fund the team at first. Institutions, grants or the founder's own resources must cover that time.
