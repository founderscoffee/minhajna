# 0017. National totals may inform exam scope, under three safeguards

- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Decided by:** the lead maintainer, after at least 30 days of comments, because this changes the charter

## Context

Charter point 7 (PRD §1.15) says that aggregates are never used for exam thresholds or personnel decisions. The charter changes only through the process for changing a principle. [0011](0011-insight-figures-review-and-limits.md) made the same promise for the opt-in insights.

Algeria has set exam "thresholds" from progress collections before, for the baccalaureate in 2014 and 2020. In April 2026, correspondence 50 had CEM directors report the last unit taught in each subject, to adjust the exams (research 08, 09, 11). So the Ministry collects progress data with or without Tabachir, and those collections name each school.

Decision record 0015, proposed alongside this one, lets the national system form wilaya and national totals of how far classes got, without the server learning any school's or teacher's figure.

The research also warns that when figures can shrink an exam's scope, a class gains by reporting less (research 11). Such figures stay true only if they are checked.

On 29 September 2026 the founder chose to let wilaya and national totals inform exam scope.

## Options

- **Keep the ban,** and offer only the inspectors' sample of exercise books (PRD §8.5). It keeps the promise, but the Ministry is likely to go on collecting progress school by school, by other means.
- **Allow any use of the totals.** Simple, but nothing would check them, and nothing would limit them to the wilaya and national level.
- **Allow wilaya and national totals only, under safeguards.** Chosen.

## Decision

- **Charter point 7 becomes:**

  > **Aggregates only above the school.** Minimum sizes and methods are published in advance. Aggregates are never used for personnel decisions. Only the national system's wilaya and national totals of how far classes got may inform the scope of exams. They are taken on a date announced at the start of the school year, published with how they were used, and checked against the inspectors' sample of pupils' exercise books.

- **Never below the wilaya.** No figure for a directorate, a school, a class or a teacher informs exam scope. Every total meets the minimum group sizes of [0013](0013-insights-payload-and-minimum-group-sizes.md).
- **The three safeguards:**
  1. **A date set in advance.** The totals are taken on a date announced at the start of the school year, never through a sudden collection.
  2. **Published.** Each time, the totals used, their date and how they shaped the scope are published.
  3. **Checked.** Inspectors compare pupils' exercise books with the texts book in a random sample of classes, twice a year (PRD §8.5). The results are published with the totals.
- **What the national system enforces.** It forms the totals for exam scope only on the announced date, and publishes every one of them, so no unpublished figure from it can be used. The Ministry is asked to adopt all three safeguards in its text, as a request, not a condition (0015).
- **The opt-in insights keep their ban** (0011). Figures from teachers who opted in are never used for exam scope.

## Consequences

- **Only charter point 7 changes.** The change stays open for at least 30 days of comments (PRD §1.2).
- **The risk remains, and the safeguards answer it.** If teachers believe that reporting less shrinks an exam, some may. But the totals come from the signed weekly records, which are official and always show corrections, and the inspectors' sample checks them.
- **Two of the safeguards depend on others.** The sample depends on the IGP, and publishing how the totals were used depends on the Ministry. The project builds the system to support both, and asks for them.
- **The rest of the PRD is brought in line:** §1.6, §2.6 and §8.4 keep the ban for the opt-in insights and point to charter point 7 for the national totals.
